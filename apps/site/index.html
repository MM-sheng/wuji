01 · The step

Every block,
one step.

Nobody quotes this index. Each Bitcoin block hash is hashed once more, its thirty-two bytes are summed, and the sum's distance from the middle moves S. The whole rule fits in five lines.

02 · Nothing to trust

No one decides it.
No one can stop it.

I

Bitcoin's work

Steering S means throwing away blocks, and every block thrown away costs its miner the reward. The cost is paid in real hash power.

II

Your own browser

This page recomputes every step from public block hashes and matches the contract to the last digit. You don't have to believe us.

III

No one's keys

No owner, no admin, no upgrade, no token. If nobody submits headers, it waits. It never computes a wrong value.

03 · The contract

Three rules.
Nothing else.

01

Only valid Bitcoin headers

Proof of work, difficulty retargets, timestamps and linkage, checked exactly as Bitcoin checks them.

02

One hundred confirmations

A height counts only once a hundred valid blocks follow it: Bitcoin's own maturity for new coins.

03

A deep reorganization seals it

If Bitcoin ever rewrites a counted height, anyone can prove it, and the index freezes for good on its last consistent value.

05 · Keep it alive

Anyone can carry it.

A keeper reads headers straight from the Bitcoin network and submits them. It watches for deep reorganizations and seals the index by itself. Fees pay keepers once the reserve holds any; the more of them, the less WUJI depends on anyone.
